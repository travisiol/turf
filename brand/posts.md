# TURF — 3 posts for X

Attach `banner-1500x500.png` or a screenshot of the map to post 1. Replace `[link]` and `$TICKER` before posting.
Everything below is what the contract does; nothing promises a return.

## 1 — What it is

100 turfs. That's the whole map.

Take one by paying its price in ETH.
While you hold it, it earns 1/100 of every trading fee on $TICKER.
If someone takes it from you, you get your price back plus 10%.

Take a turf. Earn from every trade.
[link]

## 2 — What you gain

Two ways a turf pays you:

1. Every trade of $TICKER sends ETH to the game. It is split over the 100 turfs. Your turf's share is yours to claim, any time.
2. When someone takes your turf, they pay 20% more than you did. You get your ETH back + half of that increase.

No admin. No lockup. The rules are in the contract.
[link]

## 3 — How to play

How TURF works, in 4 lines:

→ Pick a turf on the map. You see its price, and what you'd get if someone takes it from you.
→ Pay the price. The turf is yours.
→ Fees from every trade land on it. Claim when you want.
→ Free turfs keep what they collected while empty. The first taker gets it.

Turfs start at 0.001 ETH.
